Given a set of points $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ in $d$-dimensional space, the $K$-means problem
aims to find a clustering of the points into $K$ groups as to minimize the sum of squared
error from the points to their cluster centers. Formally, the $K$-means problems aims to infer
a clustering $C : [n] \rightarrow [K]$ and a set of centroids $\mu_1, \ldots, \mu_K$ such
that:

$$\sum_{i=1}^n\lVert x_i - \mu_{C(i)}\rVert^2_2$$

is minimized.
