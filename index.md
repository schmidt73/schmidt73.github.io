I am a current Quantitative Researcher at [Squarepoint Capital](https://www.squarepoint-capital.com/) and 
a recent PhD graduate from [Princeton
University](https://www.cs.princeton.edu). During my PhD, I was very fortunate to be advised by
[Ben Raphael](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/people/profile/braphael), with whom I 
tackled problems in phylogenetics and machine learning.

In detail, I applied linear and convex optimization techniques to
large-scale phylogenetic problems in cancer evolution ([fastBE](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012631))
and ancestral reconstruction ([tree labeling polytope](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.14.638328v1.abstract)).
In my final year, I applied similar tools to design constant-factor approximation
algorithms for [low-rank optimal transport](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/poster/62477).
I also worked on a handful of practical problems during my PhD. Most notably, I designed a tool for 
[CRISPR guideRNA design](https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13059-025-03488-8)
that played a *very small* role in correcting a disease-causing genetic variant in a seven month-old patient,
see the 
[New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/09/opinion/genetic-editing-diseases-health-care.html) coverage of the 
story.
